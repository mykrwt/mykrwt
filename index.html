A little about me

I'm mykrwt — a web developer who cares a bit too much about the small stuff. The way a page loads, how a button feels under your thumb, the rhythm of good typography. Those details are the fun part for me.

Most of my days are spent with modern JavaScript, semantic HTML, and CSS that doesn't fall apart as a project grows. I love working closely with designers, obsessing over accessibility, and shipping things I'm actually proud to put my name on.

When I step away from the keyboard, you'll usually find me sketching layouts, reading about how systems fit together, or chasing the perfect cup of coffee.
